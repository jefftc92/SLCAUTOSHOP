The Problem
The Murray location page (/locations/murray-ut-auto-repair) has a services section
at the bottom. Every service link in that section points to the OLD service URLs:
"Clutch Repair in Murray" → /services/clutch-repair-south-salt-lake-ut   ❌ WRONG
"Brake Service in Murray" → /services/brake-service-south-salt-lake-ut   ❌ WRONG
These should point to the NEAR-CITY versions:
"Clutch Repair in Murray" → /services/clutch-repair-near-murray-ut       ✅ CORRECT
"Brake Service in Murray" → /services/brake-service-near-murray-ut       ✅ CORRECT
This is critical. When Google crawls the Murray location page, it follows the
links to discover new pages. Right now it follows them to pages it already knows
about. It never discovers the near-city pages from this path.
